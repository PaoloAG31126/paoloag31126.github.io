CV anonymization — FAQ

Practical answers to common questions about anonymizing a CV or resume for a blind application, including what tools like CVAnon actually do and don't do.

What does anonymizing a CV mean?

Anonymizing a CV means removing the information that lets a reader identify you before the merit of your work is assessed: your name, email address, phone number, social links, location (city/country) and, where possible, age-related phrases. The goal is a fairer, blind-style review.

Why should I anonymize my CV or resume?

Many employers use blind application to reduce unconscious bias around name, age, gender, location or school. In the EU, GDPR also encourages you to share only the personal data strictly needed. Anonymizing is a quick way to protect yourself and to signal that you are a fair-process advocate.

Is it legal to send a CV without my name?

In most cases yes, especially for public-sector and some private-sector blind hiring. The recruiter may still need your identity at a later stage. Check the employer's instructions first; where the form requires a name, you can use initials or a candidate code.

What exactly does CVAnon remove?

Email addresses, social media handles and profile links, phone numbers, age phrases like "38-year-old", and common city and country names. It also tries to match common first names followed by a capitalized surname. Review the result before sending: a tool can't see context the way you do.

CVAnon removed a city or name I wanted to keep. Why?

The rules are intentionally conservative to avoid missing identifiers. If something was removed by mistake, tweak the original text (e.g., rephrase "based in Milan" as "available in EMEA") and run the tool again. The output is always yours to edit.

Does it remove my name?

It tries. CVAnon looks for a list of common first names followed by a capitalized surname. Unusual names, names written in all caps, or names in the middle of a sentence may not match. Treat the tool as a strong assist, not a guarantee: read the result before you send it.

Does CVAnon store my CV?

No. The text lives only in the page you have open. Once you close the tab, it is gone. There is no database, no log and no third party.

What is "blind hiring" and does it help me?

Blind hiring (or anonymized recruitment) is a process where the interviewer can't see identifying details like name, age, gender or school. Research shows it can reduce bias. Whether it is mandatory at your target employer varies — but it is a growing practice, especially in the EU public sector.
